About the Game

Code Quest: Bugs & Ladders is a competitive 2-player strategy board game inspired by the classic Snakes and Ladders—where luck alone isn’t enough to win.

Face off against another player in a race to reach tile 100, but every move depends not just on the dice… but on how well you think under pressure.

Game Elements

2-Player Gameplay

  • Designed for head-to-head competition.
  • Take turns rolling the dice and answering questions.
  • Outsmart your opponent and gain the advantage.

Core Mechanics

Before every move, a question appears with a strict 8-second timer.

  • Correct Answer
    Move forward based on your dice roll.
  • Wrong Answer / Timeout
    You still roll… but move backward instead.

Game Features

  • Classic 100-tile board with:
      Ladders (boost you forward)
      Bugs (send you backward)
  • Smooth tile-by-tile movement animation
  • Bounce-back rule when exceeding tile 100
  • Dynamic turn-based system

Player Customization

  • Choose from 8 unique characters
  • Enter your own player names
  • Each player has a personalized player card UI
